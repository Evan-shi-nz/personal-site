Small numbers still count. A first payment, a first band score, a first message from a stranger who found the site.

Most of the time the hard part is not knowing what to do, it is doing it consistently when nobody is watching.

## What worked

- Short sessions, every day
- Writing answers by hand first
- Checking against band descriptors
